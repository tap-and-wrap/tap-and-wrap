# Build Your Gift Box V2 — owner packing worksheet

Complete this sheet from actual packing trials. Every box starts **pending**. Blank entries mean unknown, never unlimited or approved. The artwork and photo-study outlines show a visual arrangement only; they do not establish usable dimensions, lid clearance, packing capacity or safe transport.

Record the exact builder IDs, not an ordinary Product name or SKU. Roses use `rose-red`, `rose-yellow` and `rose-black`; toys use `plush-1`–`plush-17`; chocolates use `chocolate-1`–`chocolate-20` and mean one complete pictured retail unit. Record the exact ribbon ID and whether `gift-card-1` is compatible. Builder inventory, including chocolate, remains separate from ordinary Product inventory.

Record an explicit zero mixed limit if a combination is prohibited. Do not use a large limit to mean “not tested.” Keep category and total limits conservative enough to cover permitted combinations; reduce them or leave approval pending when evidence is incomplete.

The current v1 validator applies each mixed limit whenever **at least two of that rule's listed categories are present**, and counts all units in those listed categories. A three-category limit therefore also caps each two-category mixture. A zero three-category limit prohibits every mixed-content selection, including pairs. Pair-specific limits still apply alongside the three-category limit; the most restrictive applicable count wins. This is a conservative count contract, not a list of exact tested SKU combinations.

The evidence must support the entire envelope of combinations permitted by compatible IDs, individual/category/total limits and every applicable mixed rule. If v1 cannot express the intended restriction safely, keep the policy pending. Do not broaden approval beyond tested evidence or alter the validator to make a trial appear compatible.

## Record the decision in administration

1. Save a draft for the exact box. Enter compatible IDs, explicit quantities, region permissions, combined limits and packing instructions. Unfilled information remains pending.
2. Review the entire saved draft against this sheet. Verify the current catalog/asset/reference versions and confirm the physical trial covers the permitted combinations.
3. Approve only through the explicit administrator approval action, with packing, transport and lid checks attested and a specific evidence description. The server records the administrator, time, immutable version and content fingerprint. The record is the owner's attestation; the computer has not measured or certified fit.
4. A changed approved rule requires a new reviewed version. Withdraw the active policy promptly if its assumptions, supplied components or packaging change. Retain earlier trial evidence and order references.

Physical-policy approval does **not** approve artwork or photo geometry, publish media, provision inventory, activate Add to Cart or open checkout. Those release decisions remain separate. See the [release checklist](GIFT_BOX_BUILDER_V2_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md).
